# Terza Support

Thank you for using Terza.

Terza is a simple and convenient currency converter designed to help you quickly calculate currency conversions using current exchange rates.

## Need Help?

If you have a question, found a problem, or would like to suggest an improvement, please contact us:

**Email:** terzaapp.help@gmail.com

When reporting a problem, please include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the issue.

## Terza Pro

If you experience an issue with a Terza Pro purchase or subscription, you can also manage your subscriptions through your Apple ID settings.
