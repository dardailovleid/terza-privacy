# Privacy Policy for Terza

**Last updated: September 20, 2026**

Terza is a currency converter application designed to provide currency conversion, exchange-rate information, calculator functionality, favorites, history, alerts, and related features.

This Privacy Policy explains how Terza handles information when you use the application.

## Data Collection

Terza does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data about its users.

The application does not use advertising SDKs, third-party advertising services, or cross-app tracking technologies.

Terza does not require users to create an account or provide personal information in order to use the application.

## Local Data

Certain information required for application functionality may be stored locally on your device. This may include application settings, selected currencies, favorites, conversion history, custom currency settings, and other preferences.

This information remains on your device and is not collected by the developer.

You can remove locally stored application data by deleting the application from your device.

## Exchange Rate Data

Terza connects to external exchange-rate services in order to retrieve currency and exchange-rate information.

Network requests required to provide this functionality may necessarily transmit technical information such as an IP address to the service provider as part of normal Internet communication. Terza does not use this information to identify users or create user profiles.

## In-App Purchases

Terza may offer optional paid features or subscriptions through Apple's In-App Purchase system.

Purchases and payment information are processed by Apple. Terza does not receive or store your full payment information.

## Notifications

If you enable notifications or currency alerts, Terza may use Apple's notification technologies to provide those features. Notification permissions can be managed through your device settings.

## Analytics and Tracking

Terza does not use third-party advertising trackers and does not track users across applications or websites owned by other companies.

## Children's Privacy

Terza does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its features change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Terza, please contact the developer through the support contact provided on Terza's App Store page.
